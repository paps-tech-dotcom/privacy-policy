TeaBuzz Privacy Policy

Learn how TeaBuzz collects, uses, protects, and handles information when you use the app.

Last Updated: February 27, 2026

1. Introduction

TeaBuzz ("App", "we", "us", or "our") is a social platform designed for university and college students. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the App.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account registration details (username, email address, password)
  • Profile information (name, school, profile picture)
  • Messages and communications
  • Media files (photos and videos)
  • Location data (only if you choose to share it)
  • Marketplace listings and transaction-related information

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App
  • Enable social, messaging, and community features
  • Process marketplace interactions and transactions
  • Send notifications, updates, and service-related communications
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encryption and access controls, to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only when required by law, to comply with legal processes, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the App.

6. Third-Party Services

TeaBuzz may use third-party services to provide functionality such as authentication, data storage, analytics, notifications, and other app services. These services may collect or process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Third-party service providers may change as TeaBuzz develops. Where applicable, their services are used to help operate and maintain the App.

7. Children's Privacy

TeaBuzz is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and review your personal information
  • Request corrections to inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

9. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

blackishtechnologies@gmail.com

For general TeaBuzz assistance, please visit our TeaBuzz Support Page .
